⚖️ Code of Conduct
⛔ Content — Immediate Removal
  • ⛔Racial discrimination, extreme violence, or explicit material.
  • ⛔Plagiarism or unauthorized material use.
  • ⛔Disparaging other games to boost your visibility.
  • ⛔Claiming to be official Marvel Rivals staff or representatives.
  • ⛔Hacking, cheating tools, unapproved software, or bug exploits.
  • ⛔AI-generated content submissions.
💬 Discord Community Guidelines
  • ✅Keep interactions positive, friendly, and playful.
  • ✅Embrace diversity — no racism, harassment, or exclusion.
  • ✅Review and follow the Discord Server Guidelines.
  • ✅Support fellow Climbers — no disruptive or negative interactions.
  • ✅Showcase your creation once per day in the showcase channel.
  • ⛔No promotion of game mods or unauthorized modifications.
🎮 Gaming Account Guidelines
  • Always use a valid, lawful account for content creation.
  • Never share, lend, borrow, buy, or sell game accounts.
  • ⚠️ Violations may result in warnings or permanent game account bans.
📤 Exiting the Program
  • Contact a Rivals Climbers Program Helper in Discord during working hours.
  • The team will process your request and guide you through the exit steps.
🎧 Feedback & Support
  • DM @Climber-helper in Discord for questions, concerns, or suggestions.
  • We periodically collect feedback to keep improving the program.
❓ FAQ
Can I tell others I'm a Rivals Climbers creator?
Yes! You're welcome to share that you're part of the Rivals Climbers Program.
Are there monthly content quotas?
No fixed quota. 1 month inactive → reminder DM. 2 consecutive months inactive → removal from the program.
What happens if I violate the guidelines?
First unintentional offense: warning, no penalty. Repeated or serious violations: removal and termination of collaboration. You may appeal if you believe an error was made.
Do I keep ownership of my content?
Yes — you retain full ownership. If Marvel Rivals wants to reuse your work, they'll reach out via Discord DM or email for permission first.
Who do I contact for unresolved issues?
Reach out to the Rivals Climbers Program Helper in the Discord server.
